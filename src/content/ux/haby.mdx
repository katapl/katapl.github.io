One of the requirements for passing the "iOS Application Development" class I took in university was a group project.
Me and my teammate chose to create a habit tracking app, as we were both interested in self-development and felt
that no apps available catered to our needs.

The user can choose to create habits of these three types: deadline, on-time and amount and can choose whether
the habit has to be completed daily or weekly. The app keeps a "streak" of consecutive days where habits have
been completed, to motivate the user to keep going. Notifications are sent to remind the user of their habits
both on mobile and in the Apple Watch counterpart app.

You can find the code for the app here: https://github.com/KrazyManJ/Haby
