This design was created as a part of an assignment in a web design class. I created a simple template for an online store selling music records and merch.

### Assignment
The goal of the assignment was to learn the basics of Figma (focusing mainly on the use of components, auto-layout and prototyping)
and how to create a design system.

The first step was specifying the purpose of our website and describing its target group.
Then we were encouraged to research websites similar to our own, collect inspiration and create moodboards.
Afterwards we created a design system and wireframes before moving onto the final design.
The final step of the assignment was user testing. We were taught proper techniques - how to choose a group of test users and
how to guide them through the testing.

### The result
I decided to create a website for a store selling records and band merch. The target group for this website would be music
enthusiasts and fans of alternative musicians who enjoy owning physical media.

Many indie and lesser known bands and musicians sell their records on their own websites,
and are usually not sold in bigger record stores. There is also a rising trend of owning
more physical media among generation Z, as well as the fact that many audiophiles prefer to listen to physical records.

The website is kept minimal on purpose in order to let the artwork of the album covers and colored vinyls shine.
That is also why the color scheme is mostly in black and white.

Because this design is so minimal, it can be easily used as a template for websites of different purposes.
