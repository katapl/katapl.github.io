A task that was assigned to me as part of my university internship was redesigning a website for
a youth group organizing camps and other outdoor events for kids.

### Research
My first step was asking a member of the organization about the requirements of the redesign. I was told they wanted to mainly
update the website design and simplify the web. Afterwards I researched websites of similar organizations
and studied their architecture, layouts and visual style.

### Web architecture
Part of the task was simplifying and updating the web's architecture. I wanted to make sure the site was
comprehensible for newcomers and provided needed information for existing members of the organization about upcoming events.
The web would also serve as a gateway into the organization's interal information system.

<img class="image-in-text" src={arch.src} style="width: 720px" />

### Design
I decided to organize all of the subpages into groups and made sure they were all accessible through a topbar.

In terms of visual style, I wanted to make sure the web fits with the organization's logo, icons and custom font. That is why I
used rounded corners and primary colors taken straight from the logo.
The organization also has an extensive gallery of photos from past events, so I decided to utilize those as well, for the banner,
thumbnails of past events, illustrative images in the 'about us' subpage and homepage.
