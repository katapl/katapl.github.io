This redesign was done as a small design exercise to try and clean up the homepage of a small brewery's website.

Currently the website serves as a source of information about
the business and an online store. I asked the owners and they said most people order by phone or in-person, so the online store
is practically unused.

I removed unused subpages (store, account, latest updates), and I also removed the old logo since it was
primarily used as a bottle label and is not fit to be used as a website logo.

Overall I wanted to clean up the web and made sure it fulfills its primary purpose - to allow customers to contact the business
to buy their products, and it's secondary purpose - to let customers learn about the business and its products.
